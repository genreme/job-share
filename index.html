AI-powered job application workflow. Use these prompts with Claude for each application phase.

FOUNDATION

Start Here (Do Once)

⏱️ 1-2 hours

Build your reusable Application Profile, develop positioning, and create tracking systems.

Profile Builder Prompt: 
I'm setting up my job application profile. Please interview me to build a reusable summary I can paste into future application chats.

Ask me about (one topic at a time, conversationally):

1. CURRENT/RECENT ROLE
   - Title, company, years in role
   - Team size and scope (budget, reports, reach)
   - Sector/industry

2. KEY ACHIEVEMENTS
   - 3-5 major projects or accomplishments
   - Metrics and outcomes for each
   - What made each significant

3. CAREER TRAJECTORY
   - How I got here (brief arc)
   - What I'm known for / my reputation
   - Leadership philosophy in 1-2 sentences

4. TARGET ROLES
   - Role types I'm pursuing (titles, level)
   - Company types that interest me (size, sector, stage)
   - What I value most in a role (mission, growth, autonomy, etc.)

5. KNOWN GAPS OR PIVOTS
   - Any career transitions I'm navigating
   - Gaps I expect to address (industry, skills, etc.)

After the interview, compile everything into a structured "Application Profile" I can save and reuse.
APPLICATION PROCESS

Per Role — Assessment Through Materials

⏱️ 2-3 hours per role

Complete for each opportunity. Start a fresh chat, paste Application Profile, attach resume, include full JD.

Phase 0: Fit Assessment Prompt: 
I'm evaluating a job opportunity. Please help me assess fit before I invest time in a full application.

CONTEXT:
- My Application Profile: [PASTE OR REFERENCE]
- Job Description: [PASTE FULL JD]
- Company: [NAME]

ASSESS:
1. ROLE FIT (40%)
   - Does level match my experience?
   - Are core responsibilities aligned?
   - Would I be underqualified or overqualified?

2. COMPANY/MISSION FIT (30%)
   - Alignment with my values and target sectors
   - Company stage/size/culture signals

3. PRACTICAL FIT (30%)
   - Location/remote policy
   - Compensation signals (if available)
   - Growth potential

OUTPUT:
- Fit Score: X/100
- Go/No-Go Recommendation with reasoning
- If GO: Key positioning angles to emphasize
- If NO-GO: Why not worth pursuing
Phase 3: Resume Tailoring Prompt: 
Using the research and positioning from our conversation, help me tailor my resume for this role.

ATTACHED: My baseline resume (PDF)

REQUIREMENTS:
1. Keep overall structure and format
2. Adjust Professional Summary for this specific role
3. Reorder/emphasize bullet points based on JD priorities
4. Add keywords from JD naturally (ATS optimization)
5. Keep to 1 page (or 2 if truly senior)

OUTPUT:
- Tracked changes summary (what you modified and why)
- Full revised resume text
- ATS keyword checklist
Phase 4: Cover Letter Prompt: 
Using everything we've developed, write my cover letter for this role.

REQUIREMENTS:
1. 3-4 paragraphs, under 400 words
2. Opening: Hook that connects my experience to their specific situation
3. Body: 2-3 specific examples that demonstrate fit (use STAR lite)
4. Closing: Clear call to action, enthusiasm without desperation

TONE: Confident, warm, specific. Not generic or overly formal.

DO NOT:
- Use phrases like "I am writing to apply for..."
- Repeat the resume verbatim
- Be vague or generic
- Sound desperate or overeager

OUTPUT: Full cover letter ready to submit
INTERVIEW PREPARATION

Role-Specific Positioning Through Practice

⏱️ 1-2 hours per interview

Once invited to interview, adapt your positioning and prepare thoroughly for each conversation.

Interview Prep Prompt: 
I have an interview scheduled. Help me prepare comprehensively.

CONTEXT:
- Role: [TITLE at COMPANY]
- Interview with: [NAME, TITLE] (if known)
- Interview type: [Phone screen / Video / In-person / Panel]
- Date/Time: [DATE]

USING OUR PREVIOUS RESEARCH, PREPARE:

1. LIKELY QUESTIONS (10-15)
   - Role-specific technical questions
   - Behavioral questions (STAR format answers ready)
   - Company/culture fit questions
   - "Tell me about yourself" tailored to this role

2. MY QUESTIONS FOR THEM (5-8)
   - About the role and team
   - About challenges and success metrics
   - About culture and growth
   - Strategic questions that show I've done research

3. KEY TALKING POINTS
   - My 60-second intro for this specific role
   - 3-4 stories that demonstrate fit
   - How I address any potential concerns (gaps, transitions)

4. LOGISTICS PREP
   - What to wear (based on company culture)
   - Tech check reminders (if video)
   - Materials to have ready
POST-INTERVIEW

References Through Offer Decision

⏱️ 30-60 min as needed

Prepare references, negotiate compensation, and make the final decision with confidence.

Thank You Email Prompt: 
Help me write a thank you email after my interview.

CONTEXT:
- Interviewed with: [NAME, TITLE]
- Key topics discussed: [2-3 main conversation points]
- Something specific they mentioned: [A detail to reference]
- My strongest moment: [What went well]
- Any concern I want to address: [Optional - something I could have answered better]

REQUIREMENTS:
- Send within 4-6 hours of interview
- 3-4 short paragraphs
- Reference specific conversation points
- Reinforce my fit without being repetitive
- Express genuine enthusiasm
- Clear and professional close

OUTPUT: Email ready to send
Offer Negotiation Prompt: 
I received an offer. Help me evaluate and prepare negotiation strategy.

OFFER DETAILS:
- Base salary: $[AMOUNT]
- Bonus/Commission: [DETAILS]
- Equity: [DETAILS if applicable]
- Benefits: [KEY BENEFITS]
- Start date: [DATE]
- Other terms: [ANYTHING NOTABLE]

MY CONTEXT:
- My target range was: $[FLOOR] - $[TARGET]
- My current/recent comp: $[AMOUNT]
- My BATNA (other options): [DESCRIBE]
- Must-haves vs nice-to-haves: [LIST]

HELP ME:
1. Evaluate the total comp package
2. Identify what to negotiate (salary, bonus, equity, start date, etc.)
3. Prepare negotiation talking points
4. Draft the negotiation email/script
5. Prepare for their likely responses
